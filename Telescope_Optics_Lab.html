    Prescription

    The surfaces in the order light meets them. Radii are signed along the bench: negative when the centre of curvature lies towards the incoming light.

    #SurfaceRadius, mmConic K / asphereTo next, mmThenClear ⌀, mm

    How the numbers are made: every picture comes from tracing rays through the actual surfaces, with conic mirrors, aspheric plates and catalogue dispersion for the glasses (Schott N-BK7, F2 and N-BAK4, and calcium fluoride). The spots are geometric, so diffraction appears only as the Airy circle for scale; a spot is marked diffraction-limited when its RMS radius is smaller than the Airy radius. Mirrors and lenses are sized to pass the field you set. Corrector plates and doublet radii are solved in the page. The Maksutov and Modified Dall–Kirkham prescriptions were optimised for this lab and are not any maker’s design.
